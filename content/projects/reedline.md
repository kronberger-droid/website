The line editor behind Nushell — the thing that handles your keystrokes,
history, completions, and multi-line editing at the prompt.

I work mainly on the modal editing side: the Vi mode, and a Helix edit mode
built on a selection-first cursor model rather than Vi's motion-then-operator
one.
